# Note 03 — Continuity

Euler's constant emerges from a discrete process taken to its limit.

Modern systems are the same: many small, careful steps compound into something
smooth.

```math
e = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left( 1 + \frac{1}{n} \right)^{n}
```

```math
\frac{d}{dt} e^{kt} = k \, e^{kt}
```

> Working principle: prefer compounding steps to sudden rewrites.
